Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

At Zyppwash, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you contact us or use our vehicle washing and detailing services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Zyppwash, we may collect:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages and information you share when contacting us.
  • Vehicle details, such as vehicle type and model, if provided.
  • Booking details, including service selection, address, preferred date and time.
  • Payment confirmation details or payment proof if you choose to submit them.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and customer support requests.
  • Provide information about our washing and detailing services.
  • Create, manage and confirm service bookings.
  • Communicate booking updates and service-related notifications.
  • Process or confirm payments where applicable.
  • Improve our services and customer experience.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Zyppwash may use WhatsApp Business services and automated messaging tools to communicate with customers. When you message us through WhatsApp, your phone number and message content may be processed to respond to your enquiry and manage your request.

WhatsApp is provided by Meta. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. Zyppwash does not control how Meta processes information through its platform.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers when necessary to operate our services, such as:

  • WhatsApp and Meta for customer communication.
  • Database and hosting providers for securely operating our systems.
  • Payment service providers when payment processing is required.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect our legal rights and the safety of our customers.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, provide our services, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that you have shared with us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

You can also stop receiving non-essential promotional communications by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general customers and are not directed towards children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or privacy practices. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
